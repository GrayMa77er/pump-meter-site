Pump Meter Support

Pump Meter is a fast, focused log for breast pumping: log a session in a couple of taps or with Siri, see your daily totals and trends, and share the log with your partner over iCloud.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas? Email support@pumpmeter.app. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

How do I log a session?

Tap Log pump on the Today screen, set the amount, and tap Save. The duration starts on your default length, which you can change in Settings › Logging. You can also say "Hey Siri, log pump in Pump Meter", or add the log action to Shortcuts or your iPhone's Action Button.

How do I fix or delete a session?

Open History (the list button on the Today screen). Tap a session to edit it, or swipe left to delete it. Right after a delete you can tap Undo.

Do I need an account?

No. Pump Meter has no accounts. Your sessions are saved on your iPhone and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud account so they're backed up and sync to your other devices. Settings › Data shows the iCloud sync status.

How do I share with my partner?

Go to Settings › Partner › Invite partner and send the iCloud link. Your partner installs Pump Meter on their own iPhone and opens the link. You both see and can log, edit and delete the same sessions. Use Manage sharing to remove them or stop sharing. Both phones need to be signed in to iCloud.

What does Pump Meter do with Apple Health?

If you connect Apple Health from the Charts screen, Pump Meter reads your sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and steps to show how they line up with your yield. It never writes to Health, and this data stays on your iPhone: it isn't synced or shared with your partner. An insight appears once you have 14 days with both Health data and pumping logged. To turn access off, open the Settings app › Health › Data Access & Devices › Pump Meter.

Can I switch between ounces and millilitres?

Yes, in Settings › Units. Every amount is converted for display; your logged sessions aren't changed.

How do reminders work?

Turn on Remind me to pump in Settings › Reminders and choose how long after your last session to be reminded. With Quiet hours on, a reminder that would fall in that window waits until it ends. Logging a session, from either phone, restarts the countdown.

How do I turn off the celebrations?

Turn off Celebrations in Settings. Milestones are still collected while it's off.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a session in History to remove it from your devices and from iCloud. To remove everything, delete the app and, if you want, delete Pump Meter's data in iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.
