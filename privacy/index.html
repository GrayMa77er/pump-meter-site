Pump Meter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Pump Meter is an iPhone app for logging breast-pumping sessions. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. In short: the developer never receives any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Data you log

Each pumping session you log stores a start time, a duration and a yield (amount pumped).

  • These sessions are saved on your iPhone.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also stored in your own private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit, so they sync between your devices and are restored if you reinstall. That data is held by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and the developer cannot access it.

Partner sharing

If you choose to invite someone through the app's iCloud share link, the people you invite can see, add, edit and delete the shared sessions. You can stop sharing, or leave a share, at any time from the app's Settings. Sharing is handled entirely by iCloud.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Pump Meter reads your sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and step count from Apple Health to show how they relate to your pumping.

  • Health data is read on your iPhone and only used to draw charts and insights there.
  • It is never written back to Health, synced to iCloud, shared with your partner, or sent anywhere else.
  • Pump Meter does not use Health data for advertising, marketing or data mining.
  • You can turn off access at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

Siri, Shortcuts and widgets

When you log a session with Siri or Shortcuts, Apple processes your voice request as described in Apple's privacy policy. Pump Meter only receives the resulting amount and duration. Widgets show data from the app on your device.

Version check

When you open Pump Meter, it downloads a small public settings file from pumpmeter.app to check whether your version is still supported. The request carries no personal data, no identifiers and nothing you have logged. As with any website, the hosting provider (Cloudflare) briefly handles your IP address to deliver the file; the developer does not log or store it.

Notifications

Pump reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No notification data leaves your device.

Diagnostics and support emails

To help fix crashes, Pump Meter keeps Apple's crash and performance reports (MetricKit) and a short app log on your iPhone. They are kept for up to 14 days and are never sent automatically.

  • They are sent only if you tap Send Diagnostics in Settings › About. That opens an email (or the share sheet) with a file attached, so you can see what's included and decide whether to send it.
  • The file contains the app version and build, the iOS version, the iPhone model, crash and hang reports (where in the app's code a problem happened) and the last 7 days of the app's own log messages, including from before a crash (event names such as "share accepted" and error messages).
  • It does not contain your pumping sessions (amounts, times or durations), Health data, or your partner's details.

If you email support, including with Send Diagnostics, your email and anything attached are used only to reply to you and fix the problem, and are not shared with anyone.

Children

Pump Meter is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting a session removes it from your devices and from iCloud. To remove everything, delete the app and, if you want, delete Pump Meter's data in iPhone Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.
